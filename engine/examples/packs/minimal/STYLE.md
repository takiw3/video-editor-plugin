# Minimal style

Quiet and modern. Clean sans, plenty of space, one blue, almost nothing moving that doesn't need to. For founders and professionals whose message is the point. Never busy.

## Type
- Display: Inter Tight, bold. Text: Inter. Mono: JetBrains Mono.

## Colour
- Accent #2F6BFF: emphasis. Grounds: white and #111111. Flat, 8px corners.

## Captions
Plain white type with a soft shadow, a short phrase at a time, no box.

## Motion
Calm: something new every 6 to 10 seconds, camera still, no black and white.

## Sound
Light effects only, no music unless a track is handed over.

## Hi-fi
A one-line hook, words behind the head sometimes, a card only for real numbers.

## Lo-fi
Phrases landing whole, real screenshots on top, no zooms.

## Showcase
One big word scaled up. Pushes and reveals on the cuts, an effect on most beats.

## Never
- Glitches, shakes, heavy outlines, more than one accent colour.
