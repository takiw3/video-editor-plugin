# Editorial style

Reads like a good magazine: an italic serif for the words that matter, warm paper tones, one burnt-orange accent, an unhurried pace. For people who explain things and want to be taken seriously. Never loud, never cluttered.

## Type
- Display: Fraunces, italic: hooks, thesis words, big figures
- Text: Inter: captions, labels, text boxes
- Mono: JetBrains Mono: every number, unit, kicker

## Colour
- Accent #E2542B: emphasis. Grounds: paper #F5F1E8, ink #16140F. Graph paper on cards, 4px corners.

## Captions
Short phrases in a dark box, landing whole.

## Motion
Calm: entrances 0.35 to 0.5s, something new every 6 to 10 seconds, a small settle on every cut.

## Sound
Light effects, no music unless a track is handed over.

## Hi-fi
A two-line hook. Words behind the head often, a card on the points worth pausing on. One black-and-white beat on the turn.

## Lo-fi
Word-by-word captions, four words at most. Real screenshots on top, no zooms.

## Showcase
A big word stamped behind the head. Pushes, reveals and zooms on the cuts, an effect on most beats rather than all.

## Never
- Neon, glows, hard zooms, all caps.
