# Tech style

Dark, sharp and precise, with mono details and one electric green. For AI, software and data videos. Never cute, never pastel.

## Type
- Display: Space Grotesk, bold. Text: Inter. Mono: JetBrains Mono for every number.

## Colour
- Accent #37E2A0: emphasis. Grounds: near-black #0A0C10 and #EDEFF2. Graph paper on cards, 6px corners.

## Captions
A few words at a time, the word being said in a green pill.

## Motion
Normal pace: something new every 4 to 6 seconds, hard zooms on the phrases that matter.

## Sound
Normal effects: clicks, ticks, whooshes. No music unless a track is handed over.

## Hi-fi
A one-line hook. Numbers big and in mono. Cards on real data.

## Lo-fi
Three words at a time, screen recordings on top often, a zoom on what matters.

## Showcase
A big word slams in. Whips, zooms and pushes on the cuts, an effect on every beat. A prompt bar makes a good motif.

## Never
- Soft serifs, warm tones, slow fades.
