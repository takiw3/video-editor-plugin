# Clean style

Neutral and clean: a modern sans, near-black and off-white, one blue accent. A safe start for anyone, and a good base to change. Never loud.

## Type
- Display: Inter Tight, bold. Text: Inter. Mono: JetBrains Mono for every number.

## Colour
- Accent #4F7BFF: emphasis. Negative #FF5A5F: losses. Positive #35C47C: gains. Grounds: #F6F5F1 and #121314. Flat, 6px corners.

## Captions
A dark box, words lighting up as they're spoken.

## Motion
Calm: something new every 6 to 10 seconds, a small settle on every cut.

## Sound
Normal effects under the voice. No music unless a track is handed over.

## Hi-fi
A two-line hook, words behind the head often, cards on real numbers.

## Lo-fi
Word-by-word captions, four words at most, real screenshots on top, zooms on what matters.

## Showcase
A big word stamped behind the head. Every transition allowed. An effect on every beat.

## Never
- Neon, glows, emoji.
