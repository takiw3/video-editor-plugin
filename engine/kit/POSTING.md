# Writing the post

From `transcript.txt`, in the creator's voice (their `STYLE.md`, and past posts if they share them). The video is what they said, so the copy can't claim more than the video does.

`post.md` holds:

- **Three caption options**: one short (a line or two), one that opens with a question, one that adds something the video didn't have time for. Each opens with a line that makes sense
  in a feed with no context, and ends with one clear thing to do.
- **The first comment**, when it helps: a link, the resource, the extra step.
- **The keyword reply**: if the video says "comment WORD", the exact text to send people and what they'll get. If the thing they're promising doesn't exist yet, say so.
- **Hashtags**: 3 to 5 specific ones, only when asked. No walls of them.
- **On-screen text check**: anything in the video that a platform might treat as a claim (income, health, results), flagged for them to confirm.

Never: guarantee reach or views; invent numbers, testimonials or results; write in a voice they don't use; tell them the "best time to post" as if it were fact. If they ask how a video will
do, say honestly that no one can predict it and what to watch after posting (saves, shares, comments, watch time at the 3-second mark).

Repurpose, one line each, only what fits: the feed crop, a YouTube Shorts title, a carousel from the same points, a newsletter paragraph.
